fail-closed M8B/M8C integration were published.

`EVIDENCE/M7-WINDOWS-CODEX-VM105-V10-DURABLE-BLOCKED.json`.

partial setup/restart evidence cannot substitute for the complete mandatory

independent Claude-only cell may proceed while the Codex blocker is preserved.
